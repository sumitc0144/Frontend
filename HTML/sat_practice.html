Academind (Maximilian Schwarzmüller)

Academind’s React tutorials are some of the most detailed on YouTube. Max explains concepts slowly and clearly, starting from JSX, components, and props, then moving into hooks, context, and Redux. His teaching style is very structured, making it easy for beginners to follow along without feeling overwhelmed.

So, if you combine Net Ninja + FreeCodeCamp + Academind + Techsith, you’ll have a complete roadmap: basics, projects, deep dives, and interview prep.
